Advocacy

Smart rules, not a ban

We want Uganda to have workable crypto policy: protect users from scams, keep the door open for legitimate cash-out and remittance use, and avoid driving activity fully underground.

What we stand for

  • Clarity over silence. Ambiguity helps scammers and hurts honest operators and users.
  • Proportionate rules. Small peer-to-peer cash-outs should not face the same burden as large institutional platforms.
  • Consumer protection that works. Education, clear disclosures, and ways to report fraud — not only bans.
  • Room for innovation. Remittances, savings in stablecoins, and local liquidity all have real use cases in Uganda.

What we do not claim

We do not offer investment advice. We support regulation that is practical for East African realities rather than copy-paste frameworks that ignore how people actually move money here.

How you can help

Share accurate information. Report scams. When policymakers consult, show up with concrete examples — not only slogans. If you work in policy, academia, or media and want a practitioner perspective, reach out.
